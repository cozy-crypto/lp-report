How is this calculated?

Your PnL is the dollar result reported by Meteora: what you have now (or got out) vs the dollar value you put in. It includes the market moving your tokens up or down.

From liquidity compares each position with simply holding the tokens you put in: what you got back (withdrawals, plus what is still in the position if it is open) minus the tokens you deposited, plus the fees you earned. Closed positions are valued at the prices when you exited, open ones at today's prices. From the market is the rest of your PnL.

Fees kept is "from liquidity" divided by fees earned. 100% means price moves cost you nothing; 0% means they ate all your fees; below 0% means providing liquidity lost money compared with holding. Shown only when fees are at least $1.

Supports Meteora DLMM for now. Covers pools where you closed a position in the last 365 days, plus pools with open positions. If a position was swapped entirely into one token, its exit price is estimated from the edge of its price range. Tokens without a current price count as $0.
