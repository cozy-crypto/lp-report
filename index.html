How is this calculated?

For every position we compare what you got back (withdrawals, plus what is still in the position if it is open) with the tokens you put in, and add the fees you earned. Closed positions are valued at the prices when you exited, so market moves after you left don't count. Open positions are valued at today's prices. Positive means LP left you with more than holding would have; negative means holding would have been better.

"PnL in dollars" is Meteora's own number: it compares with the dollar value at deposit time, so it also includes the market going up or down.

Covers pools where you closed a Meteora DLMM position in the last 365 days, plus pools with open positions. If a position was swapped entirely into one token, its exit price is estimated from the edge of its price range. Tokens without a current price count as $0.
