How is this ranked?

Every day we take the Meteora DLMM pools with the most fees in the last 24 hours (TVL over $100k), find the wallets with open positions there, and build each wallet's receipt for the last 365 days, the same way as on the main page.

Fees kept is what liquidity earned after price moves, divided by the fees earned. 100%: price moves cost nothing. Below 0%: holding the tokens would have done better. Wallets with less than $100 of fees, less than $1,000 deposited, or fees under 0.25% of what they put in are left out (there the ratio is mostly noise), and so are wallets in more than 300 pools (mostly bots).

✓ marks wallets whose owner proved them by signing a message on this site.

Pools show, among the LPs we saw in each pool, how many beat holding and the share of fees the typical (median) LP kept. These are public on-chain positions; this is not a recommendation to copy anyone or to use any pool.
