Terms of use and privacy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

What this site is

LP vs Hold is a free, read-only analytics tool. It shows how liquidity positions of a public Solana wallet on Meteora DLMM performed compared with holding the deposited tokens. It does not connect to your wallet, does not ask for keys or signatures, and cannot move funds.

Not financial advice

Everything shown is a calculation on historical public data. It is not investment, financial, tax or legal advice, and not a recommendation to provide liquidity, buy, sell or hold any asset. Past results do not predict future results. Decisions you make are your own.

Accuracy

Data comes from the public Meteora API as-is. It can be incomplete, delayed or wrong, and some prices are estimates (for example, when a position was swapped entirely into one token, or a token has no current price). The site is provided "as is", without warranties of any kind. To the extent the law allows, we are not liable for any loss arising from use of the site.

Privacy

The wallet address you enter is sent from your browser directly to the Meteora API to fetch public on-chain data. We do not run a server that receives it, and we do not store addresses, results or any other data. The address appears in the page link so you can share your result; anyone with the link can see the same public data. We use no cookies, no analytics and no tracking.

Meteora handles requests under its own terms and privacy policy. Wallet addresses are public on the Solana blockchain, but an address can be linked to a person, so share links with that in mind.

This site is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Meteora or any protocol it mentions. Names are used only to describe the data shown.
