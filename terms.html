Terms of use and privacy

Last updated: October 25, 2026 (leaderboard)

What this site is

Shoulda Held? is a free, read-only analytics tool. It shows how liquidity positions of a public Solana wallet on Meteora DLMM performed compared with holding the deposited tokens. It does not connect to your wallet, does not ask for keys or signatures, and cannot move funds.

Not financial advice

Everything shown is a calculation on historical public data. It is not investment, financial, tax or legal advice, and not a recommendation to provide liquidity, buy, sell or hold any asset. Past results do not predict future results. Decisions you make are your own.

Accuracy

Data comes from the public Meteora API as-is. It can be incomplete, delayed or wrong, and some prices are estimates (for example, when a position was swapped entirely into one token, or a token has no current price). The site is provided "as is", without warranties of any kind. To the extent the law allows, we are not liable for any loss arising from use of the site.

Privacy

Checking a wallet. The address you enter is sent from your browser directly to the Meteora API to fetch public on-chain data. To count checks we store a one-way hash of the address (not the address itself) with its result: fees kept, fees earned, amount deposited, PnL, number of pools and positions, how long the check took, and whether it failed. The address appears in the page link so you can share your result; anyone with the link can see the same public data.

Sharing on X. When you press "Share on X", we store the result card you see on the page together with the wallet address, so the link you post can show that card. Anyone with the link can see it.

Joining the waitlist. If you join, we store your email and/or X handle, the option you picked, the wallet you checked and its result (fees kept, fees earned and amount deposited), and the date. We use this only to contact you about the copy-LP launch and to understand who is interested. We don't sell or share it. The data is stored with Cloudflare, our hosting provider. To see or delete your entry, email hello@shouldaheld.com.

Analytics. We count page visits ourselves: a visit is stored with its date, the site you came from (if your browser reports it), whether the screen is phone-sized, your browser language and, if you came from a shared card, that card's id. No IP address, no cookies, no tracking across sites, no advertising.

Leaderboard. Once a day we compute results for public wallets that hold open Meteora positions in busy pools and publish the ranked ones with a shortened address, using only public on-chain data. To have your wallet removed, email hello@shouldaheld.com.

Meteora handles requests under its own terms and privacy policy. Wallet addresses are public on the Solana blockchain, but an address can be linked to a person, so share links with that in mind.
